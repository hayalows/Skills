## 1. One-page opportunity brief

**Desired result:** [observable outcome, review date]

**Beneficiary:** [who benefits and what they need]

**Constraints:** [time, cost, financial floor, obligations, non-negotiables]

**Current strengths:** [skills and actual evidence]

**Target:** [person/company/user group and why it fits]

**Observed need:** [fact and dated source]

**Hypothesis:** [interpretation, confidence, missing evidence]

**Useful sample:** [artifact and completion criteria]

**Effort cap:** [hours/cost; what triggers expansion]

**Contact route:** [recipient and reason for this route]

**Ask:** [one question or next step]

**Review:** [success signal, change condition, stop condition]

**Today:** [one action]

## 2. Experiment card

- Question: [what this attempt will teach]
- Hypothesis: [expected observation, not a guarantee]
- Action: [specific bounded attempt]
- Artifact: [what will exist]
- Time/cost ceiling: [limits]
- Baseline/comparison: [what the result can be compared against]
- Feedback source: [reviewer or rubric]
- Review date: [date or explicit relative interval]
- Continue if: [evidence]
- Change if: [evidence]
- Stop if: [evidence or violated constraint]
- Actual result: [fill only after doing it]

## 3. Opportunity comparison

| Option | Hard limits pass? | Learning | Earning | Network | Story | Evidence/confidence | Unknowns |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current path | [yes/no/unknown] | [0–10] | [0–10] | [0–10] | [0–10] | [reasons] | [facts needed] |
| Option A | […] | […] | […] | […] | […] | […] | […] |
| Option B | […] | […] | […] | […] | […] | […] | […] |

State weights if used. Explain the decision in words. Do not choose a boundary-violating option because its arithmetic is highest.

## 4. Work-sample brief

**Observation:** [specific issue and source]

**Audience/task:** [who needs to do what]

**Proposed improvement:** [actual work]

**Rationale:** [why this may help]

**Assumptions:** [unknowns]

**Test:** [method, baseline if available, outcome measure]

**Status:** [draft/mockup/tested prototype; actual checks]

**Next step:** [small scoped continuation]

**Rights/access:** [original/authorized materials and limitations]

## 5. Cold proposal

Subject: [Specific work] for [company/project]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [verified observation] while [honest context]. I thought [narrow opportunity] might be worth testing.

I made [sample] showing [specific change]: [link or attached artifact]. It's based on [public/supplied material], so [important limitation if needed].

Would [one small next step] be useful? If so, I can [defined follow-through].

[Name]

Remove the limitation sentence if it adds no material information. Do not imply the sample guarantees results. Check the link and recipient before any authorized sending.

## 6. Introduction request

Hi [Connection], do you know [Target] well enough to ask if they'd be open to an introduction? I prepared [sample] addressing [relevant problem], and I think there may be a fit for [contribution]. No problem if an introduction wouldn't be appropriate.

Here's a short note you could forward:

“[Name] prepared [specific sample] for [relevant task]. They'd like to ask [one clear question or opportunity]. Here's the work: [link]. Would you be open to an introduction?”

Do not put claims into the connection's mouth that they cannot substantiate.

## 7. Feedback request

Hi [Name], I've tried [actual attempt] and I'm unsure about [one component]. Here's [brief artifact]. From your experience with [relevant domain], is [specific question]? A short answer would help me decide what to change.

## 8. Feedback result update

Thank you for the suggestion about [specific issue]. I changed [actual change] and found [observed result, including uncertainty]. It helped me [truthful learning].

Add a further question only if needed; gratitude need not conceal another ask.

## 9. Value-adding follow-up

Hi [Name], I spotted [new relevant observation] and updated [small part of the earlier sample]. The change is [specific improvement], shown here: [link/artifact].

If [same scoped opportunity] is relevant, I'm happy to [next step].

[Name]

Do not manufacture new value to fill a follow-up schedule. Stop when appropriate.

## 10. Public pitch outline

**Ask:** I'm looking for [specific contribution/opportunity] with [target or type of organization].

**Who:** My background is [verified relevant facts].

**Proof:** Here's [actual work or documented result].

**Reason:** [Specific observed fit and source if appropriate].

**Extra work:** I prepared [useful original artifact], showing [improvement].

**Next step:** [single clear ask].

Keep wording natural rather than publishing the labels mechanically. Make unofficial status clear.

## 11. Honest case study

- Situation: [who, task, constraints]
- My contribution: [specific work and boundaries of responsibility]
- Evidence: [sources, dates, calculations or checks]
- Result: [what was observed, not what was hoped]
- Limits: [causality, sample size, missing data, untested assumptions]
- Learning: [what changed in understanding]
- Next test: [specific next step]

## 12. Weekly review

**Completed:** [actual artifacts/attempts]

**Inputs:** [hours/cost; relevant approaches]

**Outputs:** [responses/conversations/trials/results, with denominators]

**Best work:** [up to three items and why]

**Weakest work:** [up to three items and why]

**Main bottleneck:** [evidence, uncertainty]

**One change next week:** [action]

**Continue/change/stop:** [decision and reason]

## Reusable prompts

- “Use $create-opportunities. I have [skills], [hours per week], and [constraints]. Help me choose one [job/client/business] opportunity and prepare a small useful sample.”
- “Use $create-opportunities to audit [target]. Research current public sources, separate facts from hypotheses, and recommend one contribution I can actually demonstrate.”
- “Use $create-opportunities to compare these offers. My financial floor is [amount/currency], my non-negotiables are [list], and the evidence I have is [facts]. Include staying on my current path.”
- “Use $create-opportunities to write a short outreach draft from this sample. Use only my verified background and give the recipient one easy next step. Don't send it.”
- “Use $create-opportunities to turn this artifact into a public case study. Label hypotheses, protect private information, and avoid overstating impact.”
- “Use $create-opportunities to review this ledger. Find the likely bottleneck, state uncertainty, and recommend one cheap change.”
