# Humanize My Writing

Write like a thoughtful person speaking to another person. Preserve meaning, facts, emotional temperature, and the user's level of confidence.

## Scope and companion skills

Use this skill primarily to repair an existing draft. Preserve technical meaning, required quotations, qualifications and commitments before improving rhythm. When writing as Papa Kojo, use the bundled voice profile; if Papa Kojo Writing Style is also active, let its specific voice guidance govern. Use Human Copywriting for offer structure only when persuasion is part of the request. Do not activate every writing workflow for a small edit.

## Choose the mode

- **Write:** Draft from notes or a request in the user's voice.
- **Diagnose:** Explain what feels artificial, quote only the problem fragments, and rank the issues by impact. Do not rewrite unless asked.
- **Rewrite:** Return the finished copy first. Explain changes only when useful or requested.
- **Diagnose and rewrite:** Give a short diagnosis, then the finished version.
- **Learn:** Extract stable voice patterns from user-confirmed samples and apply them in the current work. Treat corrections and explicit preferences as stronger evidence than raw dictated text.

If the requested mode is clear, start working. Ask one focused question only when the audience, purpose, or factual meaning would materially change the result.

## Core workflow

1. Identify the audience, purpose, medium, and desired action.
2. Separate the user's meaning from the draft's wording. Never sacrifice a useful idea merely to shorten the copy.
3. Apply the voice profile. Match tone to the setting: casual messages can sound looser; applications, church talks, and business writing need cleaner grammar without losing warmth.
4. Remove generated-writing tells. Prefer concrete nouns, active verbs, plain transitions, and varied sentence lengths.
5. Read the result as speech. Fix stiff phrases, repeated sentence shapes, fake drama, abrupt fragments, and lines the user would be unlikely to say.
6. Check factual claims, names, dates, quotations, and links. Do not invent supporting detail.
7. Deliver the requested output without announcing the method.

## Voice-learning rules

- Learn patterns, not typos. Do not copy transcription errors, repeated filler caused by speech, or misspellings.
- Preserve intentional local expression when the audience will understand it. Do not manufacture Ghanaian slang or dialect.
- Prefer confirmed edits and repeated preferences over one-off samples.
- Infer cautiously. If a sample conflicts with the established profile, match the current context instead of rewriting the profile in your head.
- Never claim perfect imitation. Aim for recognizable continuity while keeping each piece appropriate for its audience.

## Output rules

- For short drafts, provide only the copy unless the user requests analysis.
- For diagnosis, use a compact table or bullets with: pattern, quoted evidence, effect, and fix.
- For long-form writing, let the argument develop through cause, consequence, and deeper cause. Use whitespace to control pace.
- Preserve necessary technical terms and explain them plainly.
- Do not flatten personality in pursuit of correctness.
- Avoid em dashes in newly written prose. Preserve required exact quotations and proper names.

## Meaning comparison

Compare the original and revision for actor, action, date, amount, status, certainty and emotional intensity. Mark genuine ambiguity rather than silently choosing a more impressive claim. Do not add deliberate errors or promise to bypass an AI detector. Finish when the identified weaknesses are repaired.

## Final check

Ask:

- Does this sound like a person with a real point, or a model performing good writing?
- Can the reader picture the situation?
- Did any sentence announce importance instead of showing why it matters?
- Are there canned openings, neat triples, fake quotations, vague praise, or business jargon?
- Did the rewrite preserve the user's meaning and emotional intent?
- Would the user plausibly say this aloud?
