# v1.2.1.1 Threadline Trial: exploratory results (16 Nov 2026)

**Status:** DRAFT only. Do not merge into the approved writing skill on `main`.

## What changed

The candidate preserves the approved v1.2.1 skill and adds one optional module for substantial reflective essays, long talks and complex explanations. It privately maps the reader's questions and reviews whether the opening, middle and ending build the same thought. Short forms are intentionally excluded.

**GitHub blob for approved v1.2.1:** `21b0e2a5989aab8842849f8e92ba622adb1975cc`. No official source file was modified.

## Verification

- 11/11 direct GitHub integrity checks passed: official baseline intact; all original seven writing principles present; no fabricated study claims; longform-only scope; truth safeguards retained.
- 14/14 local source/format acceptance checks passed, including comparing an exact Git-hash copy of v1.2.1 and verifying eight archived short-format negative controls are identical *by construction*. Such replays are not generated candidate outputs.
- Two fresh longer illustrative pairs were drafted in **one assistant session**. This does not count as independent calls and cannot establish general or causal superiority.
- The same voice-checker was used on all illustrative passages. No human voice ratings or blind test took place.

Readability is a rough surface metric, **not** a score of soul, human naturalness, authentic voice, or factual support. A lower score does not mean a worse essay. The candidate was more concrete in the work/rest opening, but its money essay at times made the outline visible in phrases like “There is a reasonable objection here” or “The next question”. That is a credible downside of adding planning instructions. A user-avoided word was found and corrected in the candidate test essay before this report.

## Editorial assessment

**Do not promote v1.2.1.1 yet.** The optional procedure is coherent and passed structural checks, but these tests supply no evidence that it is substantially better than v1.2.1 for Papa Kojo's voice. More explicit planning is not automatically more natural. The owner should only consider promotion after identical fresh tasks, truly isolated repeated model generations, truth audits, and direct reader preference evidence. Treat this trial as an implementation smoke test and hypothesis exploration.

The full texts, mechanical output JSON and checks are retained in the accompanying downloadable experiment package in the ChatGPT conversation. No new results have been added to the existing Writing Blind Lab.
