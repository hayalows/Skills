# Threadline Trial 01: thought continuity add-on

**Status:** experimental method only; this is not an approved new voice or a trained model. Source: Threadline 0.1 at commit `8ce39a8fea22e4e085db2a3079b987cfa7f898be`.

## Only for substantial writing

For long reflective essays, longer talks and complex explainers (roughly 500+ words), plan the reader's questions before drafting. Do not use this extra procedure for WhatsApp, simple emails, captions, applications, apologies, short rewrites or quick factual answers. The official voice profile, approved v1.2.1 example, and user's current instructions take precedence.

## Private thought map

Write a single honest central claim and map the minimum reader questions needed to reach a reasonable conclusion. For each substantial paragraph or section, ask privately: What is this answering? What does it add (reason, mechanism, concrete example, consequence, objection, answer)? Why here? What evidence is actually supplied, what is unknown, and what must remain hypothetical? Arrange the map so new paragraphs develop rather than restart the point. The map is internal; no compulsory visible headings, three-part structures or stock transitions.

## Opening, middle and ending

Choose an opening that is relevant and factual. An ordinary detail or hypothetical scene can help, but no fabricated first-person memories, emotion or striking fact. After writing, compare representative opening, middle and ending paragraphs against the same central claim. Check for repeated claims and changed meaning. Remove transitions that announce rather than connect ideas. Finish where the argument honestly finishes; do not add a polished slogan or forced callback.

## Guardrails

Do not shorten every sentence for readability, force a scene, add a token objection or extend a short message. For rewrites, check that claims, degree of certainty, person and future commitments are identical to the source. Automated repetition or Flesch flags are advisory. A high score is not evidence of voice authenticity. Do not promote the module without controlled independent generations, factual audits and the owner's approval.
