# Campaign planning, delivery, measurement

Define goal, recipient group and exclusion criteria, location and applicable rules, offer, available evidence, list source, contact details, suppression, review owner, sending method, follow-up schedule, reply ownership, and stop conditions. Start with a small, manually reviewed pilot; do not prescribe a universal sending volume or guaranteed outcome. Separate commercial solicitation from job, research, and partnership contact, and check local law and recipient/provider rules for the exact purpose and jurisdictions. A publicly available address does not by itself establish consent or legal permission. Do not store unnecessary sensitive data. Keep source, date, status and suppression; minimize retention and access.

Before sending at scale, verify current sending-provider requirements and relevant official legal guidance. Google sender guidelines: https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126 ; FTC US commercial-email guide: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business . Rules vary across countries, message types and provider thresholds; do not assume US rules apply everywhere or that compliance with law overrides provider policies. Gmail discourages unsolicited mail and purchased lists. For eligible bulk promotional traffic, current provider requirements can include one-click unsubscribe. Identify the sender, use truthful subject lines, provide and honor appropriate opt-outs, configure SPF/DKIM/DMARC where required, and check actual domain alignment. Do not promise inbox placement or use automated fake engagement. Reduce or stop sending on bounces, complaints, deferrals, opt-outs, or a provider warning; investigate before resuming.

Track unique recipients, sends including follow-ups, delivered estimate, hard bounces, complaints if observable, total replies, positive qualified replies, meetings held, opportunities, deals and effort/cost. Label definitions and time window. Examples: positive reply rate = unique recipients who positively replied / unique recipients contacted; meeting rate = unique recipients who booked / unique recipients contacted. Do not confuse sends with unique recipients or booked with attended. A small sample carries large uncertainty; opens are unreliable. Test one meaningful variable at a time with comparable randomly assigned recipients where feasible, record the offer/segment/date, monitor negative outcomes, and avoid claiming causation from tiny or changing samples. A low response can reflect fit, offer, proof, timing, deliverability, or copy; investigate rather than assume one cause.
