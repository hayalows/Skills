# YouTube source matrix

## Scope and ranking method

- Window: **2026-03-06 through 2026-09-06 UTC**.
- Queries covered UI/UX design, UX research/usability, UI/UX redesign, UI/UX trends, and AI for UI/UX.
- The 20 rows are the strongest public results returned by those searches after removing obvious duplicates and videos outside the date window. “Top” is therefore a reproducible search sample, not an official YouTube-wide ranking. Views and recency below were recorded when visible and can change.
- Transcript evidence was collected from YouTube’s own transcript surface when available and a public transcript utility when YouTube did not expose usable captions. YouTube says the transcript view depends on captions being available: [YouTube Help: View video transcripts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15930243?hl=en). The utility used for capture was [Tactiq’s YouTube Transcript Generator](https://tactiq.io/tools/youtube-transcript). The skill keeps paraphrases and source links, not full third-party transcript text.

## Reliability notes

The sample mixes high-reach explainers, course material, redesign critiques, trend reports, AI workflow videos, and case studies so that “good UI/UX” is not reduced to one visual style. Counts in `ui-ux-evidence.md` are based on the 10 transcripted rows only. Missing-caption rows are retained in the scope and used only for public metadata-level claims.
