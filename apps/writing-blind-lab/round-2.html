How this test works and what it can prove

Both options answer the same brief. Each writing version appears twice on each side over the complete round, and the seven writing tasks appear twice. Your choices stay anonymous until the full round ends. The quick stage helps you start, but only the optional second stage completes the balanced design. Even 14 comparisons provide early preference evidence, not a scientifically reliable definitive ranking.

These are pilot drafts produced by one model following different writing instructions, not independent model runs. The test measures which passages you prefer, and does not automatically measure truth, authorship or writing quality. Your original 24-question study remains separate and unchanged.
