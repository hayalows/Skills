# Craft sources and selective application

Research checked on 2026-09-22. These are craft influences, not evidence of Papa Kojo's own voice. The techniques below are editorial adaptations. Do not impersonate the writers, copy their signature phrasing, or use their popularity as proof that a technique works for every reader. The user's meaning and preferences take priority.

## Dan Koe: give an idea room to develop

Source: [How to articulate yourself intelligently](https://letters.thedankoe.com/p/how-to-articulate-yourself-intelligently), 20 November 2025. Article text reviewed.

Koe describes practising a small body of recurring ideas and expanding a central point with support. He also presents problem-led short narratives and connections between fields. Adaptation: identify a thought worth expressing, explain it with relevant evidence, and return to meaningful themes through fresh examples. Borrow a cross-field comparison only when it makes the mechanism clearer. Do not copy creator-economy hype, universal prescriptions or arguments from audience size. A clever analogy is not proof.

## Alex Hormozi: make usefulness and reader relevance concrete

Sources: [How I Write: Alex Hormozi: Copywriting Masterclass](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/alex-hormozi-copywriting-masterclass/id1700171470?i=1000689400622), 5 February 2025; [Hormozi's own show republishing the interview](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/why-my-books-go-viral-david-perrell-interview-ep-856/id1254720112?i=1000708440485).

Evidence boundary: reviewed publisher episode descriptions and chapter markers, not the full audio or a verified verbatim transcript. These identify clarity, usefulness, structure, audience, and the customer's problem as topics. Adaptation: make the reader's practical difficulty recognisable, explain the offered help, and make the next step clear. Do not attribute a detailed formula or direct quotation to Hormozi on this evidence. Do not import aggressive sales pressure, invented guarantees or sensational business claims. Further technique-level claims need the primary audio or transcript checked first.

## Russell M. Nelson: connect teaching to conduct

Source: [Peacemakers Needed](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2023/04/47nelson?lang=eng), April 2023 general conference. Official talk text reviewed.

The talk connects an opening personal experience to a moral concern, scripture and specific interpersonal behaviour. Adaptation: for church writing, explain how the teaching changes an everyday response and offer a clear, compassionate invitation. Keep dignity while addressing conduct honestly. Do not borrow Nelson's experience as the user's, manufacture a testimony, copy his institutional authority or carry a sermon cadence into business emails. Verify any quotation in the official source at drafting time.

## Paul Graham: make the sentence sound spoken

Sources: [Write Like You Talk](https://www.paulgraham.com/talk.html) and [Writing, Briefly](https://paulgraham.com/writing44.html). Essay texts reviewed.

Graham recommends conversational language, revision and reading aloud. Adaptation: test whether the writer could comfortably explain the thought to an intelligent friend, then cut wording that obscures it. Preserve the precision needed by the topic. Spoken clarity is compatible with professional writing; copying stumbles and filler is unnecessary. Allow revision to improve the idea as well as the sentence, while preserving the user's actual position.

## George Orwell: identify what the phrase actually means

Source: [Politics and the English Language](https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/politics-and-the-english-language/), 1946. Estate-authorised text at the Orwell Foundation reviewed.

Orwell examines stale phrasing, vagueness and language that conceals meaning. Adaptation: name the actor, action and real consequence before choosing polished wording. Ask what a metaphor helps the reader picture. Remove a stock phrase when it substitutes for a thought. Do not turn his advice into absolute bans on useful technical vocabulary or passive voice when the actor is unknown or irrelevant.

## Use the influences proportionately

| Writing job | Most useful technique |
| --- | --- |
| Short message | Spoken clarity and correct relationship context. |
| Reflective post or essay | One considered idea, causal development, honest complication. |
| Offer or service page | Reader problem, practical result, available proof, clear next action. |
| Church talk | Concrete application, trustworthy teaching, compassionate invitation. |
| Editing any draft | Meaning before polish, read-aloud revision, removal of empty wording. |

No source establishes that these choices always sound like Papa Kojo. Judge that through his corrections and explicit approval over time. Keep paraphrases brief and source-linked; do not build a corpus of copyrighted passages into the skill.
