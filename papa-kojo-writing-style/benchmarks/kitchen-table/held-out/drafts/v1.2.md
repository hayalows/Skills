## H05
When the business and the owner share one till, it gets hard to tell how either of them is doing.

Many small business owners take money from the till, or from the business MoMo, whenever something comes up. Transport, school fees, a family need. It feels fine, because it's your business and you're the one who built it.

The trouble shows up at the end of the month. You try to work out how the business did, and you can't. Some of the money left as personal spending, and nobody wrote it down. A slow month and a month where you took out a lot look the same in the till.

Paying yourself a fixed amount changes that. You decide on a figure, you pay yourself that figure on a set day, and the rest stays with the business. Now when money is short, you know the cause is in the business itself, and you can look for it.

It helps at home too. A fixed amount is something you can plan your household around.

It isn't easy at first, especially when sales go up and down from week to week. So in this issue, we'll look at how to pick an amount you can keep paying, and what to do in a month when the business can't cover it.

## H06
In Mosiah chapter 4, King Benjamin teaches his people to care for the poor, feed the hungry and visit the sick. That is a lot to ask of anyone. Then, in verse 27, he adds this: "And see that all these things are done in wisdom and order; for it is not requisite that a man should run faster than he has strength."

I think this verse speaks to how we change.

When we decide to do better, we often want to fix everything at once. We see all the ways we fall short and try to repair them in a single week. Then we slip, and we feel we have failed.

King Benjamin doesn't tell his people that the work doesn't matter. He tells them to do it in wisdom and order. A runner who goes faster than his strength allows will tire long before the end of the race.

So being patient with ourselves is part of changing. We can choose one thing to work on, keep at it, and add more as our strength grows. When we fall short, we can start again the next morning.

This week, maybe think of one change you are working on. Ask yourself if you are running faster than you have strength. If you are, it is all right to slow your pace and keep going.
