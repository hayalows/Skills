# Kitchen Table Benchmark: 15 fresh briefs (frozen 2026-11-18)

Write each piece as Papa Kojo Mensah (Iconka), from Kumasi, Ghana. Use ONLY the facts given in each brief. Do not invent stories, numbers, names, quotes, feelings or experiences. If something is unknown, leave it out or use a clear placeholder like [time].

## B01 WhatsApp reply (20-60 words)
His friend Kwesi asks to borrow GH₵500 until month end. Papa Kojo cannot lend it this month because his own rent is due on Friday. Decline kindly and keep the friendship warm.

## B02 LinkedIn comment (10-35 words)
Akosua, a former colleague from Absa, posted that she has started a new role as a relationship manager. Comment on her post.

## B03 Email (60-120 words)
Write to Mr Boateng, the venue manager for Pitch Night on 13 November. Ask if the setup time can move from 4pm to 2pm because the sound check needs more time. Ask whether the earlier time adds any cost.

## B04 LinkedIn post (120-200 words)
Papa Kojo reviewed 150 designer portfolios for a project called Portfolio Field Notes. Facts from his notes: the strongest portfolios explained the problem before showing visuals; weaker ones were galleries of images with no context; several made it hard to find contact details. Share what he noticed and one useful takeaway for designers.

## B05 Newsletter opening, Clarity Before Growth (150-220 words)
Topic: why a shop can be busy every day and still be short of cash. Causes to explain: stock bought on credit, customers who pay late. Readers are small business owners in Ghana. No client stories are given.

## B06 Church talk section (180-260 words)
A short part of a sacrament meeting talk on keeping the Sabbath day holy when work is busy. Given scripture: Exodus 20:8, "Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy." No personal experiences are given.

## B07 Condolence message (25-60 words)
Brother Mensah from church lost his father. Papa Kojo plans to attend the funeral on Saturday. Send a message.

## B08 Apology email (60-120 words)
To Ama, owner of a bakery and a design client. Her logo is three days late because Papa Kojo was ill. The new delivery date is Thursday. Apologise and confirm the date.

## B09 Short invitation copy (50-100 words)
For StigmaClear's Letters page (Nkra): invite visitors to write a kind letter to a stranger going through a hard time. Facts: it takes about two minutes; every letter is reviewed before it appears; writers must not include names, phone numbers or links.

## B10 Essay (300-450 words)
Should young people in Ghana start a side business while they are employed? Give an honest view with reasons on both sides, one real objection, and when it may not be wise. No personal story is given.

## B11 Rewrite into a clear update (50-110 words)
Turn his rough note into a message to a friend who is helping test the app. Keep every fact and every level of certainty:
"so the routelab app now has the tracking thing working but only when the app is open, background tracking is not done yet, i think by next month maybe we can try the native version but not sure"

No metrics or awards.

To Sister Grace, his BYU-Pathway mentor, after he finished the Hospitality and Tourism Management certificate. She checked in with him during the course.
