# Source catalogue for natural writing craft benchmarks

Research compiled 16 November 2026 using the **secondary Firecrawl connection**. This catalogue records our interpretation of writing methods from publicly available pages. It does not copy their full work, train a writing model, assert their approval, or certify what any of these people would score on our rubric. Each URL is provided so the claim can be checked. Sources are not interchangeable genres.

## Research-to-criterion traceability

- **Truth and voice:** Gary Vaynerchuk, Ann Handley, Paul Graham, Austin Kleon/Zadie Smith.
- **Reader and clarity:** Harry Dry, Paul Graham, Nielsen Norman Group, James Clear.
- **Reasoning, specificity and transitions:** Alex Hormozi (episode description), James Clear, Russell Brunson.
- **Emotion and pace:** Ann Handley, Gary Vaynerchuk, Brunson (genre-qualified).
- **Anti-template:** Paul Graham, Harry Dry, Handley, paired with the user's own explicit dislike of certain phrase patterns.

**Important:** This list is a heterogeneous, editorial sample rather than a representative author corpus. It was selected for useful methods and cannot establish statistical similarity to any one author. Some pages are summaries or compiled quotes; source quality is marked rather than silently counted as primary prose.
