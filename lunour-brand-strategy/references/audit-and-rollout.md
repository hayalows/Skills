# Brand audit and rollout reference

Use this file when diagnosing a current brand, choosing refresh versus rebrand, or planning adoption after launch.

## Touchpoint inventory

Record the current state of:

- company name and descriptor
- website and landing pages
- search results and directory profiles
- social profiles and recurring post formats
- sales deck, proposal, invoice, and email signature
- product UI, onboarding, notifications, and error messages
- customer support and service scripts
- packaging, signage, events, and printed materials
- recruitment, internal communication, and partner materials

For each touchpoint record owner, audience, purpose, current asset, last update, and known problem.

## Audit matrix

Score 1 to 5, then write evidence and root cause.

| Touchpoint | Clarity | Distinctiveness | Credibility | Consistency | Usability | Scalability | Accessibility or cultural fit | Root cause |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
|  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |

Do not average away a serious problem. A legal, cultural, accessibility, or trust issue can outweigh a higher visual score.

## Refresh or rebrand decision

### Favour a refresh when

- the position and audience are still right
- customers recognise and trust the existing identity
- the main problems are dated execution, inconsistent use, or weak templates
- the name and core promise still fit the future

### Favour a rebrand when

- the offer, audience, category, or ambition changed materially
- the current name or message creates repeated confusion
- the visual identity signals the wrong market, price, or level of trust
- the company has accumulated conflicting assets that a patch cannot fix
- a major market or product expansion requires a new frame

State what will be lost, what will be preserved, and how the change will be explained.

## Governance

Define:

- one source of truth for guidelines and assets
- one brand owner and backup owner
- who approves strategy, copy, design, and legal changes
- naming and version rules
- how requests are submitted and reviewed
- what can be self-served from templates
- a review cadence for new channels and products
- how exceptions are documented

The goal is controlled autonomy. People should move quickly inside clear boundaries.

## Measures

Use a mix of leading and lagging signals:

- message comprehension in short user interviews
- unaided and aided recall
- qualified enquiries and conversion by source
- sales objections about difference or credibility
- consistency and template adoption
- time required to produce a new asset
- accessibility defects and support confusion
- customer, partner, and candidate comments about perception

Do not attribute every commercial change to branding. Record the period, comparison, other changes, and level of confidence.
