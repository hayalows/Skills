# Intentional outreach and follow-up engine (v1.2)

This is for **explicit outreach**, not normal replies. Student data stays in authorised workplace tools, not this public repository or unauthorised AI. An outreach draft is not proof of sending.

## Plan before writing

Check the current official PC or C&D Weekly Guide for its *actual* week, dates, theme and resources. Then check the authorised student timeline, previous contact, unanswered message, Required Action, contact channel, student local time and any commitments. The relevant focus for a previous week is not automatically correct now. Treat old templates as copy examples only, not policy or evidence of contact.

### Mutually exclusive tracks

- **Never contacted:** Introduce the mentor once, briefly state the current week’s relevant purpose, ask one easy question tied to the weekly milestone. Don't assume registration, goals, device, class access or problem.
- **Previous contact, no student reply:** Follow up only when current guidance and timing support it. Avoid "you haven't replied" guilt or repetition of the entire original email. Respect preferences.
- **Student replied and mentor hasn't:** This is **REPLY**, not an outreach campaign. Prioritise the pending answer.
- **Problem being worked on:** Message must address the known obstacle or agreed next step; do not switch to a generic grade check.
- **Student already registered/completed the milestone:** Ask only about the next useful verified milestone if it makes sense; no redundant question.
- **Student declined contact / stopped:** Respect the decision and authorised outreach policy; do not press.
- **Required Action assigned:** Read its specific guide and response key first; do not treat it like a free-form ordinary outreach.

## Compose for the person and the week

Write in v3.0 Original Message register for WhatsApp, the appropriate Email register for email. Use a real purpose, a small practical request and a clear end. Don't force emoji, "I hope you are doing well" every time, a long speech, meeting link or more than one main question.

Never invent the exact weekly focus, deadlines, 77% target, scholarship eligibility or preferred channel. Verify those when needed. Don't imply that the student is falling behind unless evidence supports it.

### Practical follow-up contract

A proposal is not an agreement: "Would it help if I checked on Thursday?" = **offered**. Student accepting = **agreed**, and the mentor must follow through. A scheduled meeting is not an attended meeting. A reminder without contact is not sent outreach. Maintain an authorised record of pending promises and due dates.

### Email specifics

Use subject relevant to verified week, the correct recipient and the **approved account signature** rather than inventing or duplicating it. Do not copy a full work signature into a live WhatsApp continuation. No mass BCC roster or third-party campaign uploads unless expressly permitted by official guidance.

## Five pre-send checks

- Is there a student reply or promise that deserves attention first?
- Is the week's theme and programme actually verified?
- Does the message match the current student's known stage rather than a fictional one?
- Is the requested action doable and within mentor scope?
- Is drafting/sending permitted under the current AI Use Policy, contact time rules and user authorisation?

Do not auto-send or write to Portal/Sheet. The mentor decides and records verified real actions.
