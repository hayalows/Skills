# Finding credible BYU-Pathway and Springboard guidance

**Purpose:** Make the skill useful beyond wordsmithing. Before advising about policy, procedures, deadlines, grades, support, enrolment, aid or referrals, proactively **find, open and read the most relevant current primary source**.

**These links are discovery pointers, not cached policies.** The article text, interface, eligibility or deadline may change. Reopen the relevant current page each time a consequential claim depends on it.

## Source order

1. Current official Springboard Mentor Handbook, Mentoring Hub and relevant PC/C&D Weekly Guide, Required Actions, AI Use Policy, official HSEF and Mentor Bridge guides. Internal authorised guidance may contain rules not on the public web.
2. Official BYU-Pathway **Knowledge Base** for exact student steps, current **Academic Catalog** and calendar for academic policy and dates, official student portal/Companion help for navigation.
3. Authorised Mentor Portal for actual student statuses and recommendations (subject to permission and AI policy).
4. Private user playbook/Field Guide only as background; never substitute an old copied procedure for the official live rule.
5. Web search and third-party discussion only to **find** official sources, never to establish institutional policy.

## Search process

1. Translate the student's problem into a precise query **without personal information**, such as `site:help.byupathway.edu "Contacting an Instructor on Canvas"` or `site:help.byupathway.edu "Tuition Discount" "KA-02641"`.
2. Prefer a page at `https://help.byupathway.edu/en-US/knowledgebase/article/?kb=KA-XXXXX&lang=en`; a verified article title and **KA number** are more useful than a vague Knowledge Base home link.
3. Open the article. Read the exact section covering the next step and check any date, scope, prerequisites, exception, approval authority and related articles. A search snippet alone may be incomplete.
4. For deadlines, read the block-specific calendar **as well as** any general day-of-block rule. Pay attention to UTC versus the student's or mentor's local time.
5. Where two official sources conflict, check update dates and scope. State the conflict, avoid firm claims, and ask the MSA/relevant official team to reconcile it.
6. Write the **student reply in v3.0 Original**, with the one action or direct answer. After it, add a short **Mentor note** containing title, exact KA number, direct clickable URL, a one-sentence rule/step, and remaining uncertainty or follow-up.
7. If a page cannot be loaded or verified, say **not verified**, don't fabricate a KA number or claim you checked it. Give a safe next step or route to authorised support.

**Never put a student's name, ID, school email, tuition balance, private quote or protected personal data in public web search, issue logs or repository files.**

## Specialised official mentoring guides

- **HSEF laptop:** https://sites.google.com/view/bloommentoring/tools-and-guides/help-start-education-foundation-laptop-guide . This is an official **mentoring guide**, not a Knowledge Base article. Check authorised interviewer access, student status, duplicate applications, qualifications and the approved interview/referral workflow. Do not assume a public link means the mentor has been approved as an interviewer.
- **Mentor Bridge tuition discount:** https://sites.google.com/view/bloommentoring/tools-and-guides/mentor-bridge-tuition-discount-guide . This is an official **mentoring guide**; use it for current mentor grade/need eligibility checks, prior award rules, Portal recommendation, required message, and Finance determination. Do not claim an award or referral was completed before verification.
- **Academic policies:** https://catalog.byupathway.edu/academicpolicies/list .
- **Help Center:** https://help.byupathway.edu/ .
- **Weekly guides and Required Actions:** open the relevant **current authorised** programme-specific guide from the Mentoring Hub, not an older screenshot.

If a title or KA number changes, use the latest verified canonical source and update this index **only with confirmed evidence**.

## Suggested mentor-facing source note

**Mentor note:** Verified: [official title] ([KA-XXXXX](direct-url), checked [date]). It says [one relevant step or condition]. **Still to check:** [programme/block-specific detail or user account step]. **Next:** [the mentor's authorised follow-up].

Do not use this paragraph in every simple student reply. No source dump is needed to ask a natural follow-up question with no institutional claim.

## Quality gate

- Does each nontrivial factual claim match an opened authoritative document?
- Did I give the **real article title, KB ID and link**, rather than a made-up citation or homepage?
- Have I distinguished official guidance, a working interpretation, and an unknown?
- Did I verify relevant dates, student programme and who approves the action?
- Is the WhatsApp reply short, in Papa Kojo v3.0, with sources placed outside it unless the student benefits from a direct link?
