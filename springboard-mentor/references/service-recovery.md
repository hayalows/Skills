# Service recovery, escalation and research completion (v1.2)

The mentor's reliability is measured by what actually happens, not how reassuring the text sounds.

## An answer owed is higher priority than a new question

1. Reconstruct what Papa promised, when, and whether he delivered it.
2. If overdue, acknowledge the missed promise once without excuses.
3. **Perform the authorised source check now**, if an official KB or guide is available; read and verify it before writing.
4. Give the student the concrete finding and safe next step, respecting what the student has already done.
5. If access fails, state exactly what is not verified and who can decide. Avoid making a second vague promise.
6. Record the actual action in the authorised Mentor Portal after it happened and arrange an agreed follow-up only if needed.

### Cases

- **Already withdrawn after late support:** Do not push the student to reverse a finished action. Verify the drop/withdrawal distinction, current date, financial implications and remaining official options. Acknowledge your earlier delay without inventing that it caused the outcome.
- **Course not visible after earlier sign-in steps:** Don't repeat the same troubleshooting. Use official course-access instructions, verify current registration status with authorised tools, and then the student Help Center/Companion support path if appropriate. A mentor cannot access the account for the student.
- **Instructor not replying:** Don't say "message them" again. Check how long ago the message went, deadline and the current instructor/academic support process.
- **Tuition hardship:** Clarify the precise problem. Mentor Bridge is a *mentor-recommendation* workflow subject to current checks and Finance approval, distinct from student-driven HJG discount application. HSEF device support has a separate interview/referral process. Do not promise anything.
- **Concern about personal safety:** Follow the current official Student in Crisis Assessment Guide and authorised urgent route immediately; don't invent emergency numbers, handle it as an ordinary case, or use AI for restricted crisis information.

## Next-step standard

A useful response answers: What can this student do *now*? Who is authorised? What would change with another reply? What does the mentor still owe? If the most recent message is only "thanks", neither a resource dump nor a meeting invitation is helpful.

## Records

Use status distinctions: researched vs unverified; recommended vs submitted; offered vs booked vs attended; RA saved vs completed; drafted vs sent; student acknowledged vs substantial conversation. No phantom actions, false grades or invented outcomes.
