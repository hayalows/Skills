# Current policy checks and mentor boundaries

This file is an **operating checklist**, not a replacement for official Springboard or BYU-Pathway policy. Source links point to public-facing guidance and may change or require authorisation. Check the current official instruction for the relevant student programme, block and week.

## Authority order

1. Springboard current Mentor Handbook, AI Use Policy, Mentoring Hub, current PC/C&D Weekly Guide, Required Actions and escalation/crisis directions.
2. Current BYU-Pathway Knowledge Base, programme-specific Academic Catalog, student portal and authorised Mentor Portal workflow.
3. Current official HSEF, Mentor Bridge and other specialised programme guidance, for their own processes.
4. User's private living playbook, Field Guide, outreach examples and tracker: helpful context, not authoritative policy.
5. Historical conversations and AI replies: not proof or policy.

If a relevant source is inaccessible, say which part has not been confirmed and give a safe next step. Avoid fabricated "official" quotes and procedures. Don't assert that a student qualifies until authorised checks are complete.

## Five-step verification routine

1. **Problem and programme:** What is happening? PathwayConnect or Certificate & Degree? Which course, block and deadline?
2. **Current primary source:** Read the correct article/guide. Compare dates and whether the rule applies to this programme.
3. **Actual student steps:** Identify portal, Canvas, support channel and prerequisites; don't improvise navigation steps.
4. **Role:** Student, instructor, grader, adviser, finance/support, mentor, interviewer or MSA? Assign the step to the person authorised to act.
5. **Limit and follow-up:** State what is known and unknown, offer one useful action, and agree a follow-up if it helps.

Use source links in explanatory notes rather than crowding a one-line WhatsApp message with citations unless a link directly helps the student.

## Source quality and citation discipline

Every consequential procedural claim should have **one directly relevant, currently read official source**. Prefer the precise official Knowledge Base article over an old summary, generic homepage, third-party page, or model memory. See [source-finding.md](source-finding.md) for verified article entry points and an evidence workflow.

For a mentor-facing explanation provide: **article title**, **KA number if one exists**, **direct article URL**, **what the source actually says**, and **what remains to be confirmed for that student's programme/block**. For Springboard-only programmes, cite the current official guide rather than inventing a KA number. Name discrepancies between sources instead of silently choosing the easiest answer.

Don't repeat outdated policy without rechecking, and don't claim to have read a page that failed to load. A good answer may be: "I couldn't verify the live deadline. Please check the current Weekly Guide or confirm with your MSA before telling the student."

## Common topics

**Grades and assignments:** Check whether work is locked, missing, late, or genuinely graded zero. Encourage a clear message to the instructor through **Canvas Inbox** when appropriate, with the specific question. Do not promise an extension, reopening, changed grade or appeal outcome.

**Registration, withdrawal and tuition:** Separate dropping from withdrawal, transcript impact from GPA, tuition responsibility from refund eligibility, and student choice from mentor advice. Verify dates for the actual block. Never invent a waiver or assume a withdrawal is reversible.

**Technical difficulties:** Check device requirements, connectivity and the official support route. Do not request credentials or log into student accounts. Do not recommend technical workarounds that bypass school rules.

**Tuition discounts and financial support:** Check current eligibility, past use, award period, deadlines and any existing support. Distinguish Heber J. Grant from Mentor Bridge. A discount considered, recommended or submitted is not an award.

**Mentor Bridge:** Current official guide: https://sites.google.com/view/bloommentoring/tools-and-guides/mentor-bridge-tuition-discount-guide . A mentor assesses academic progress, demonstrated financial need and other conditions, then submits an authorised recommendation through the Mentor Portal. Finance determines final eligibility and award. Check the guide for the current grade threshold, prior-use window, submission period and required follow-up disclaimer. Never say a recommendation was submitted until it was.

**HSEF laptop programme:** Current official guide: https://sites.google.com/view/bloommentoring/tools-and-guides/help-start-education-foundation-laptop-guide . Assistance uses an authorised mentor/interviewer interview and recommendation; students do not directly conduct their own interview. Check whether the student has a prior application (duplicates are not allowed), current programme and continuity plan, required connectivity, location, and the mentor's interviewer access. A withdrawal or break may change the assessment; check current rules, don't presume automatic disqualification or acceptance. Do not promise devices, delivery, or that the mentor already has interview access.

**Employment and career support:** Verify the actual service or live opportunity before suggesting it. Career resources and Talent Storehouse registration are not job guarantees. Don't imply the mentor can place or recommend a student to an unspecified vacancy.

**Crisis or personal safety:** Do not treat a potential crisis as routine academic coaching. Follow the official urgent assessment, safety and escalation route, and minimise sensitive data in AI tools.
